## A comprehensive brainstorm of pre-packaged 1646 cells the studio could ship.

**Date:** 2026-05-19
**Status:** Brainstorm document · expected to expand and refine quickly
**Parent:** KEEP (the platform that defines 1646)
**Cross-practice scope:** STOCK (COOK, BDRC, GROW, SPORE), KEEP, CODEX, MESH, MEAL kits, BXBX small-object

**This document:** the universe of cells the studio could design and sell, organized so MEAL kits and other curated subscriptions have a clear menu to draw from

---

## § 0 · Purpose

Every studio practice that uses 1646 cells is implicitly authoring a *catalogue* of cells. STOCK has 40+ cells across COOK/BDRC/GROW/SPORE. CODEX has the Studio Classics Series in design. KEEP has the cell itself plus mod kits. MEAL is about to launch a subscription product that bundles multiple cells together.

Without a single document that lists *all the cells across all practices*, three things keep happening:

1. The studio re-invents cells that already exist in another practice (a BDRC mushroom cell and a SPORE substrate cell could be the same physical product with different framing)
2. MEAL kit subscriptions can't be designed because there's no menu to draw from
3. The customer can't see the *full catalogue* of what 1646 lets them do

This document fixes that. **It's the studio's cell menu.** Every cell the studio has built, is building, or has identified as a candidate to build, in one place, with consistent metadata.

### Cell metadata schema

For each cell:
- **Code** — e.g. COOK-01, BDRC-09, CODEX-001, MEAL-K01
- **Name** — what the cell is
- **Contents** — what's inside the 1646 envelope
- **Target price** — at what tier (Base $1.25 to Studio $5-7 per cell, plus contents)
- **CP box fit** — does it ship in Extra Small / Small / Medium / Large
- **Status** — Live · In design · Queued · Proposed
- **Cross-references** — what other cells it pairs with

## § 10 · CP box fit summary

Critical for shipping economics. The CP flat-rate box determines what kit sizes work.

| Box | Internal (in) | Cost (CAD, 2026 est.) | Cells (practical) | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Extra Small | 8.88 × 6.13 × 3.00 | ~$19 | 1 cell | Single CODEX or single BDRC |
| Small | 14.00 × 10.38 × 2.13 | ~$22 | 3-4 cells | Solo and standard MEAL kits |
| Medium | 14.94 × 10.25 × 4.75 | ~$28 | 12-15 cells | Couple/family MEAL kits, à la carte bundles |
| Large | 15.88 × 11.75 × 7.38 | ~$33 | ~22-24 cells | Quarterly showcase, full microsystem starter |

**Pricing note:** CP flat-rate pricing has been changing — sources from 2024 show $17.99/$22.99/$29.99 small/medium/large; 2026 references suggest $19-33. The studio should verify current rates before publishing customer pricing and refresh quarterly.

**The hidden constraint:** the 2.13" height of the CP Small means only *one layer* of 1646 cells (cells are 1.23" tall). A 4-cell Small kit is laid out 2×2 in the box footprint. This is fine but limits Small to ≤4 cells.

## § 12 · The MEAL kit assembly logic

This is the operational core of the new MEAL store category. A MEAL kit is *constructed* from cells in the catalogue per these rules:

**Solo Kit (MEAL-K01, $55, biweekly):** 1× BDRC + 1× COOK. Drawn from the 16 BDRC live cells and the COOK queue. Theme-paired (e.g., Persian BDRC-08 + Lamb Vindaloo COOK-05).

**Standard Kit (MEAL-K02, $90, monthly):** 2× BDRC + 1× COOK + 1× GROW *or* SPORE. The "GROW or SPORE" position rotates seasonally — herbs in spring/summer, mushrooms in fall/winter.

**Couple Kit (MEAL-K03, $135, monthly):** 3× BDRC + 2× COOK + 1× GROW/SPORE. Two cuisines per month.

**Family Kit (MEAL-K04, $175, monthly):** 4× BDRC + 2× COOK + 1× GROW + 1× SPORE. The "full microsystem month."

**Seasonal Kit (MEAL-K05, $245, quarterly):** 6× BDRC + 3× COOK + 2× GROW + 1× SPORE. Three months of cooking in one shipment.

**Quarterly Showcase (MEAL-K06, $475, quarterly):** 12× BDRC + 6× COOK + 4× GROW + 2× SPORE. The studio's flagship subscription product — a full microsystem quarter.

### Why these ratios

BDRC is the *anchor*. It's the deepest, most-mature catalogue (16 cells live) and the easiest to cook (rice cooker only). COOK adds active-cooking complexity and longer recipes. GROW adds living herbs the customer tends over the month. SPORE adds a longer-cycle cultivation that produces 2-4 weeks after delivery. **The four practices each contribute a different temporal pattern**, and the kit composition reflects that:

- BDRC = immediate (cook tonight)
- COOK = active (cook this weekend)
- GROW = sustained (harvest over weeks)
- SPORE = delayed (harvest in 2-4 weeks)

A monthly kit gives the customer all four patterns at once. That's a *meaningful subscription product*, not just a discount on bundled goods.

## § 13 · The architectural framing

This catalogue does three things at once:

1. **It names the universe.** 101 cells, all in one place, with consistent metadata.
2. **It enables MEAL kits.** Subscriptions and à la carte bundles can now be designed against a real menu.
3. **It surfaces gaps.** STOCK COOK has 12 candidates but only 1 live. STOCK GROW and SPORE have 0 live. CODEX has 0 live. **The studio's actual production roadmap can be derived from this catalogue.**

The catalogue is also the *source of truth* for what the studio sells. Every product page, every store category, every subscription bundle should reference back to a cell code in this list. If something doesn't have a code, it doesn't exist in the catalogue. If it has a code, it exists at one of four status levels.

## § 14 · Open questions

**Q1 · Do we ship multi-cell MEAL kits as one CP box, or as multiple smaller boxes?** A 4-cell kit in one CP Small ($22) is cheaper than two 2-cell kits in two CP Extra Smalls ($38). Default to consolidation; offer multi-shipment splits as a premium option only.

**Q2 · How does the studio handle BDRC's 16 live cells vs. the kit logic that calls for "2 BDRC cells per month"?** Answer: the catalogue is wide on purpose. A 12-month MEAL-K02 subscription cycles through different BDRC cells across the 12 months. The customer never gets the same cell twice in a 12-month rotation. *That's the curation work.*

**Q3 · Should MEAL kits include the cell envelope or just the contents?** Both are possible. If the customer is already buying into 1646 they have empty cells; the kit can ship as bagged contents that the customer transfers into their own cells. If they're new, the kit ships with cells included. **Default: kit ships with cells included.** Adds $1.25-2.50 per cell to COGS but makes the unboxing experience complete.

**Q4 · How are MEAL subscriptions presented in the store UI?** Two options: (a) a separate `/meal/kits/subscribe/` page that handles all program selection in one place, (b) each subscription program gets its own product page. Default: option (a), single subscription page that handles all 7 programs as a configurator. Easier to maintain.

**Q5 · Does the catalogue itself become a CODEX?** A "M1ND.studio Cell Catalogue" CODEX — one card per cell, 100+ cards documenting the full studio output. Useful both as a customer reference AND as the studio's own internal product-line documentation. Worth queuing as CODEX-T07.
