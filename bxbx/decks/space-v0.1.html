§ 01 The thesis

Small spaces
that ship.

BXBX.Space is the productized side of the studio. Every catalogue model is sized to a piece of logistics infrastructure that already exists — a U-Haul U-Box, a cargo van, a 10-foot rental truck, a 20-foot cube. The customer buys the BXBX. The customer rents or owns the vehicle. The hard problem of moving a building, already solved by an industry that does nothing else.

Three tiers per model: Plans (you build), Kit (we cut + ship), Build (turnkey, currently Toronto only). The lowest tier ships nationally today. The highest tier ships when geography permits.

No custom work. Each model is a SKU. The catalogue is the asset.

Sized to vehicles, not to ambitions. Logistics already solved by an industry that does nothing else.

§ 05 Three tiers per model

Plans. Kit. Build.

Every catalogue model is offered at three engagement tiers. Customer picks the right one for their geography, skill, and budget.

TIER 1 · plans

Plans + drawings

Digital PDF + DWG + material sourcing guide. You source materials. You build. Ships nationally and internationally — it's a download.

FROM $95 (Dome) TO $3,500 (Kuruma)
TIER 2 · kit

Kit · precut + hardware

Lumber cut to length, hinges, hardware, fasteners, glue, finishes shipped flat-pack via commodity freight. Customer assembles.

FROM $480 (Stack) TO $12,000 (Tabi)
TIER 3 · build

Turnkey build

BXBX.Laneway delivers the finished unit, currently Toronto + GTA only. Will expand as the studio licenses production to partners.

FROM $560 (Stack) TO $95,000 (Kuruma)
Pricing CAD pre-tax. Lives in bxbx-pricing.xlsx. Tier 1 ships anywhere. Tier 3 currently Toronto-only.

§ 10 Geography of the catalogue

Plans anywhere.
Kits in North America.
Builds in Toronto.

Plans ship anywhere there is internet and a printer. Buy from Toronto, Tokyo, Tbilisi. Same product. Same price.

Kits ship anywhere commodity freight ships. Currently North America (Canada + US continental). International kit shipping comes later as freight costs and customs friction are solved.

Builds currently happen in Toronto + GTA only, via BXBX.Laneway. Expansion happens through partner-builder licensing — regional fabricators in target markets cut and assemble against our drawings, take their margin, deliver turnkey.

Geography is a strategy decision, not a brand decision. Buy what fits your location. Tier 1 anywhere. Tier 3 Toronto-only for now.
