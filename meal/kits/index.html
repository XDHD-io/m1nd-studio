Subscription programs

Twelve-month curation themes

Subscription kits aren't generic — they follow themed 12-month curation programs. The customer picks a program; each month's kit aligns with that program's arc. Customers never receive the same cell twice in a 12-month rotation.

MEAL-SUB-01

Around the World in 12 Months

One cuisine per month: Morocco, Japan, India, Italy, Mexico, Vietnam, Greece, Persia, Thailand, Korea, Spain, Levant.

MEAL-SUB-02

Grains of the World

12 months exploring grain traditions: rice, wheat, corn, barley, oats, rye, sorghum, millet, teff, buckwheat, quinoa, farro.

MEAL-SUB-03

Slow Cooked

All BDRC-heavy kits, each month a different rice cooker pattern. For the customer who's bought into the rice cooker as primary tool.

MEAL-SUB-04

The Curious Kitchen

One unfamiliar ingredient per month with 2-3 recipes built around it. For cooks who want to expand their pantry.

MEAL-SUB-05

Vegetarian Year

All-vegetarian kits across 12 months. Pulls from the substantial vegetarian portion of the BDRC and COOK catalogues.

MEAL-SUB-06

Seasonal Forager

Aligned with Toronto/Ontario seasonal produce patterns. Late summer = tomato-heavy; winter = grain-heavy and preserves.

MEAL-SUB-07

Studio Microsystem Quarterly

The 24-cell quarterly box, designed for customers running the full iDOO + rice cooker + SPORE setup. Maximum integration with studio infrastructure.

How the kits get composed

The four temporal patterns

Every MEAL kit composes across the four STOCK sub-lines because each one contributes a different temporal pattern to the customer's cooking month:

  • BDRC — immediate: cook tonight, rice cooker handles it
  • COOK — active: cook this weekend, an evening of attention
  • GROW — sustained: harvest over weeks from the iDOO hydroponic
  • SPORE — delayed: cultivate and harvest mushrooms 2-4 weeks after delivery

A monthly kit gives the customer all four patterns at once. That's the meaningful subscription product — not just a bundled discount, but a designed cooking rhythm. BDRC anchors every kit (deepest live catalogue, easiest cooking); COOK adds complexity; GROW and SPORE rotate seasonally with the year.
