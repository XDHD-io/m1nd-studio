Build practice · digital products & integration

MESH · digital products as peer-to-peer infrastructure

The studio's build practice for tools, platforms, and the connective tissue between practices. Mesh-shaped because the products talk to each other — not one monolithic platform with everything inside, but a network of focused products that compose. Years of implicit work, retroactively named.

The thesis

Why mesh-shaped

The studio's digital work has always been mesh-shaped rather than centralized. PR0J.co is a production OS for studio work. MR-KT.com is a curated creative directory. CHANNEL is a broadcast-station-in-a-box. modernmotiongraphics.com is an education platform. Each is a focused product. None tries to be everything. They share design judgment and editorial register; they don't share a single backend.

This is opposite to the prevailing 2026 SaaS architecture, where every product wants to be your operating system, your file system, your social network, and your AI assistant simultaneously. The studio's bet: focused tools that compose via standard interfaces beat monolithic platforms that try to do everything. The studio's design judgment + editorial taste + AI-assisted development = digital products that punch above their weight versus the deluge of generic vibe-coded SaaS.

In development

The MHYC matching tool

The inaugural new MESH product after the retroactive documentation phase. Built in partnership with MHYC: Svelte frontend + Mapbox + Ghost API for editorial content + vector embeddings for candidate matching + human editorial confirmation as the final filter. ~2–3 week developer sprint when MHYC has enough editorial corpus to power the matching.

Build partner The MHYC matching tool is the cleanest example of the studio's research:build pairing. MHYC produces the editorial substance (neighborhood profiles with structured match notes); MESH builds the tool that operationalizes the matching. The algorithm proposes; editorial judgment disposes.
